About your travel engine

Gemini 3.7 Flash researches with Google Search and Google Maps when useful. Your prompts and relevant itinerary are sent through the server to Google; do not include sensitive identity or payment information.

Plans and notes stay in this browser. They are not encrypted, synced or backed up. Removing browser data removes them. Google retains paid interactions for up to 55 days, subject to project settings.

Saved sourced answers are personal conversation history. Maps answers expire after 175 days and cannot be exported. Search-only answers expire after 700 days. Your notes and older legacy plans are kept.

No Gemini key belongs in this browser. If you used the old app, rotate that key in Google AI Studio. Its old local copy is removed automatically.
